Usage Guidelines

✓ Do

  • Use Deep Teal for all primary brand touchpoints
  • Use Earthy Gold sparingly for secondary emphasis
  • Maintain minimum contrast ratios (AA)
  • Use Space Grotesk for display text, Inter for body
  • Keep the logo clear space equal to the icon height
  • Use semantic colors only for their intended purpose

✗ Don't

  • Don't use Vivid Teal (#04D4DB) or Cyan (#00FFFF)
  • Don't mix brand and accent as equal-weight colors
  • Don't place brand color text on mid-tone backgrounds
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or recolor the logo
  • Don't use semantic colors decoratively
  • Don't use more than 2 type weights per section
