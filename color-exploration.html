★ Recommendation: Two-Color System

Primary (dark bg): D · Vivid Teal #04D4DB — cyan's energy with teal's soul. 10.8:1 contrast on dark = AAA.

Accessible variant (light bg): A · Deep Teal #0A7E8C — passes AA on both backgrounds. Use for light-mode text, print, formal contexts.

Together they form a natural depth ramp — shallow water to deep ocean. Same hue family (~182-186°), different registers.
