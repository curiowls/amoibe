How It Works

Three Layers of Discovery

Your agent doesn't just list your resume. It helps you discover where you're going — then finds the people and companies going there too.

🪞

Self-Discovery

Your agent guides you through proven psychology frameworks — Ikigai, Career Anchors, Holland Code — to understand not just what you've done, but what you're optimizing for.

For individuals: Find your direction through structured reflection, not just resume parsing.

For companies: Wardley Mapping, JTBD, and Three Horizons reveal what talent you actually need — not just what you think you need.
🤝

Agent-to-Agent Discovery

Your agent communicates with other agents through structured protocols — sharing context selectively, discovering synergies neither human would have found alone.

Privacy-first: Tiered disclosure — agents reveal more only as mutual fit increases. You approve every escalation.

Needs-first: Your agent leads with YOUR goals, not someone else's pitch.
⚡

Frictionless Action

When both humans say "yes," agents handle the rest — contracts, SOWs, NDAs, invoicing — drafted from context they already hold. Weeks of paperwork become minutes.

Context-aware: Documents pre-filled from both agents' understanding of the engagement.

Human in the loop: You review 80% complete drafts, not blank templates.

Sarah's agent didn't say "here's a job posting." It connected her aspirations to an opportunity she didn't know existed — with timing context no platform could surface.
